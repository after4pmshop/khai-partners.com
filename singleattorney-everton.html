Everton Jeffry Hutabarat S.H., M.H.

I am Everton Jeffry Hutabarat, an Indonesian advocate and legal professional based in Balikpapan, East Kalimantan, with more than 13 years of experience in business and corporate law. My practice covers commercial contracts, corporate litigation, legal due diligence, corporate governance, and intellectual property. Drawing on experience in both law firms and corporate legal departments, I help clients understand their legal position, manage risk, and make informed business decisions.

Throughout my career, I have advised on corporate licensing, regulatory compliance, property development, and plantation operations. My experience includes drafting commercial agreements, supporting legal due diligence for a subsidiary sale valued at approximately IDR 1 trillion, and preparing a FIDIC agreement for a palm oil mill expansion. I have also assisted clients with trademark registration and intellectual property matters.

I hold a Bachelor of Law from Universitas Kristen Indonesia and a Master of Law specializing in criminal law from Universitas Sumatera Utara. I am a licensed advocate and hold a tax lawyer certification. My approach combines careful legal analysis with an understanding of commercial priorities, with a focus on providing clear advice and practical legal support.
