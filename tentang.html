Legal counsel should provide certainty.

KHAI & Partners is an Indonesian law firm headquartered in Balikpapan, East Kalimantan, founded on a straightforward premise: that legal counsel is, at its core, an exercise in applied justice — the translation of doctrine and procedure into outcomes that withstand scrutiny, protect interest, and endure.

We advise individuals, enterprises, and institutions across eight areas of practice spanning litigation and alternative dispute resolution, corporate and commercial law, insolvency, employment, property, administrative and regulatory affairs, civil law, and intellectual property. Our method is disciplined rather than transactional: every engagement begins with rigorous factual and doctrinal analysis, proceeds through a strategy calibrated to the client's actual objective, and concludes with a result our partners are prepared to stand behind personally.

What distinguishes KHAI & Partners is not size but structure. Every matter is led directly by a partner — never delegated downward and reviewed only at its conclusion. We regard this as a matter of professional responsibility as much as of service quality: the advocate whose name is on the pleading should be the advocate who has read the file.
