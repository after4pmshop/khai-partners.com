Why KHAI & Partners

Prospective clients are entitled to ask what distinguishes one firm from another. We would offer the following, stated plainly:

• Direct partner engagement. Every matter is handled personally by a partner from intake to resolution, not assigned downward and supervised remotely.

• Doctrinal rigor. Advice is built on the applicable statute, regulation, and jurisprudence — not on approximation.

• Candor We tell clients what the law permits and what it does not, including when the answer is unwelcome, a discipline the Indonesian Advocate's Code of Ethics (Kode Etik Advokat) requires and that we treat as a professional obligation rather than a constraint.

• Breadth without dilution. Eight practice areas under one roof, each overseen by a partner who has done the doctrinal work required to advise in it competently.

• A firm built deliberately, and honestly described. We do not claim a longer institutional history than we have. What we offer instead is the direct, accountable attention of the partners whose names are on the door.

MANAGING PARTNER

Khairul Anam

S.H., S.Tr.Par., M.Par., M.H.

Khairul Anam is the Managing Partner and founder of KHAI & Partners, and a member of DPC Peradi Balikpapan, the Balikpapan chapter of the Indonesian Advocates Association (Perhimpunan Advokat Indonesia). He holds a Sarjana Hukum (S.H.) and Magister Hukum (M.H.) in law, alongside a Sarjana Terapan Pariwisata (S.Tr.Par.) and Magister Pariwisata (M.Par.) in tourism management — a combination that gives him a working fluency in both the legal and commercial dimensions of the industries most active in East Kalimantan, from hospitality and property to the region's broader resource economy.

His practice centers on civil litigation, including debt-recovery and contractual disputes, and on strategic counsel to individual and corporate clients navigating disputes that carry real commercial consequence. Khairul founded KHAI & Partners on a specific operating conviction: that a client is better served by an advocate who has read the entire file personally than by a firm whose senior name appears on the letterhead but not on the correspondence. That conviction governs how he structures every engagement he leads — directly, and from intake through resolution.
