About Us

Khai & Partners Law Firm
Firm Overview

KHAI & Partners is an Indonesian law firm headquartered in Balikpapan, East Kalimantan, founded on a straightforward premise: that legal counsel is, at its core, an exercise in applied justice — the translation of doctrine and procedure into outcomes that withstand scrutiny, protect interest, and endure. We advise individuals, enterprises, and institutions across eight areas of practice spanning litigation and alternative dispute resolution, corporate and commercial law, insolvency, employment, property, administrative and regulatory affairs, civil law, and intellectual property. Our method is disciplined rather than transactional: every engagement begins with rigorous factual and doctrinal analysis, proceeds through a strategy calibrated to the client's actual objective, and concludes with a result our partners are prepared to stand behind personally. What distinguishes KHAI & Partners is not size but structure. Every matter is led directly by a partner — never delegated downward and reviewed only at its conclusion. We regard this as a matter of professional responsibility as much as of service quality: the advocate whose name is on the pleading should be the advocate who has read the file.

Why KHAI & Partners

Prospective clients are entitled to ask what distinguishes one firm from another. We would offer the following, stated plainly:

Direct partner engagement

Every matter is handled personally by a partner from intake to resolution, not assigned downward and supervised remotely.

Doctrinal rigor

Advice is built on the applicable statute, regulation, and jurisprudence — not on approximation.

Candor

We tell clients what the law permits and what it does not, including when the answer is unwelcome, a discipline the Indonesian Advocate's Code of Ethics (Kode Etik Advokat) requires and that we treat as a professional obligation rather than a constraint.

Breadth without dilution

Sixteen practice areas under one roof, each overseen by a partner who has done the doctrinal work required to advise in it competently.

A firm built deliberately, and honestly described

We do not claim a longer institutional history than we have. What we offer instead is the direct, accountable attention of the partners whose names are on the door.

Get In Touch
