We are the best full-service law firm based in Jakarta

We advise individuals, enterprises, and institutions across sixteen areas of practice spanning litigation and alternative dispute resolution, corporate and commercial law, insolvency, employment, property, administrative and regulatory affairs, civil law, and intellectual property. Our method is disciplined rather than transactional: every engagement begins with rigorous factual and doctrinal analysis, proceeds through a strategy calibrated to the client's actual objective, and concludes with a result our partners are prepared to stand behind personally.
