Criminal Law (Hukum Pidana)

Criminal Law (Hukum Pidana)

Defense and complainant-side representation in criminal proceedings, from the investigative stage through trial, grounded in the procedural protections of the Indonesian Criminal Procedure Code (KUHAP). We regard the investigative stage — questioning, phase in which a case is most often won or lost, and we engage there rather than waiting for an indictment to formalize the matter.
